# ESI (Environmental Sensitivity Index)

NOAA-style ranking of shoreline and marine habitat sensitivity to oil.

- **Sources** — NOAA ESI, regional equivalents (e.g. Australian ESI, Arctic ESI).
- **Format** — vector polygons with sensitivity ranking (1–10) and biological/socioeconomic context.
- **Use in OORCA** — `engine/ecology/*` for ecological exposure and liability components.
